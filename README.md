## Game Features

- **Endless Vertical Climbing** — Bounce upward across continuously ascending platforms, timing jumps and dives to survive procedural heights.
- **Neon Themes and Visual Transitions** — Atmospheric neon palettes that smoothly interpolate background, platform, and ambient lighting as altitude increases.
- **Multiple Worlds and Level Progression** — Progressive milestone levels organized into distinct worlds, with dimensional portals unlocking higher worlds upon completion.
- **Lives and Respawn System** — A three-life safeguard that preserves current altitude, respawning the player on the last safe platform with a temporary countdown.
- **Collectibles and In-Game Currency** — Blue Orbs and Golden Stars scattered along the climb that reward Blues currency to build player balances.
- **Key-Based Continue System** — Exchange collected Blues for Keys in the Key Vault to revive expired runs without resetting progress.
- **Star Ratings and Rewards** — Performance ratings earned across level milestones based on climb efficiency, preserved alongside personal best records.
- **Offline Progress Saving** — Player milestones, currency, star ratings, and high scores persist locally on the device with zero network connection required.
